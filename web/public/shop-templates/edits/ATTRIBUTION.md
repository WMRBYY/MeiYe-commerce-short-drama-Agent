# Multi-shot video templates

Original footage: **RefVid (service-f8bed296.example.invalid)**.

Source collection: <https://github.com/refvid/awesome-reference-to-video-prompts>.
All original prompts, photos and video clips are licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The full license is distributed at [../refvid/LICENSE.txt](../refvid/LICENSE.txt).

MeiYeAI sequenced four distinct source clips per template, normalized the frame
to 720 × 1280 with letterboxing, transcoded to H.264 and removed audio. No source
clip is looped, repeated within a template, or slowed to reach the target length.
These are edited combinations of AI-generated footage, not original continuous
20–30 second community uploads. RefVid does not endorse these adaptations.

The clip order, measured file durations and modifications are recorded in
[sources.json](sources.json). Every clip ID links to its source page at
`https://service-f8bed296.example.invalid/<clip-id>` and its original video at
`https://service-f8bed296.example.invalid/media/templates/<clip-id>/example.mp4`.
The application exposes these links in each template's details.

The source README license was verified on 2026-11-19. Original-file checksums,
source URLs and publication dates are in [../refvid/sources.json](../refvid/sources.json).
